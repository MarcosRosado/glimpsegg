## GlimpseGG 2.0.4

### Changed

- **Patch 7.42a.** The app now starts on 7.42a, even before it can reach Steam or OpenDota.
  It was a balance patch: no new heroes or items, so nothing else in the app needed to change.
- **Glint passed its check.** The 2.0.3 version was tested on 2,416 matches it had never seen
  and passed on every position, in the match and in the match list. It stays as it is. It is
  still **experimental**: it has not been measured on 7.42a matches yet.

## GlimpseGG 2.0.4

### Mudou

- **Patch 7.42a.** O app já abre na 7.42a, mesmo antes de falar com a Steam ou a OpenDota. Foi
